Ranking System

The leaderboard uses a weighted scoring system to calculate player performance (later on as Skill Score) across multiple metrics.

1. Leaderboard Terminology

  • KDR: Kill-to-Death Ratio (PMC Kills ÷ Deaths)
  • SR: Survival Rate
  • Standing: Profile standing. Depends on various suspicious detection from the server, low trust factor or any other suspicious detections.

2. Season System

Ranked and Casual profiles, during a season cycles with unique leaderboard:

  • Duration: 1-3 months per season
  • Reset: New season automatically starts when SPT major version releases or SPT Leaderboard mod. Moderation of SPTLB Team reserves a right to change its duration at any time.
  • Hall of Fame: Top 3 players from previous season are shown on the page
  • Data: Full season archives in the dropdown menu

3. Skill Formula

Final skill score is calculated using these weighted factors (0.00-100.00 scale):

  • Kill/Death Ratio (25%)
  • Survival Rate (20%)
  • Average Life Time (20%)
  • PMC Raids (30%)
  • Best of the Best Bonus (10%):
    • Best average kills/damage/loot per raid
    • Single-raid best performances
    • Best KDR and survival rates

Leaderboard Calibration:

  • Raid Requirements:
    • 70% penalty with linear reduction until 30 PMC raids
    • Full skill score multiplier is unlocked at 80+ PMC raids
    • Small bonus for players who achieved 150+ PMC raids if Season takes longer than expected
  • Life Time Penalty: 30% penalty if average life time below 10 minutes
  • Performance Tracking: Your best historical stats are permanently recorded and contribute to score
  • Boost System: -+5% skill adjustments based on mod factors
  • Casual Players: Capped at 15.00 skill score maximum

4. Skill Tiers

Players are classified into tiers based on final skill score:

  • L- to L+: Low (0.00-34.99)
  • M- to M+: Medium (35.00-64.99)
  • H- to H+: High (65.00-83.99)
  • P- to P+: Pro (84.00-96.99)
  • G: Godlike (97.00-100)

5. Visual Indicators

Statistics, including SPT versions are color-coded based on thresholds:

  • KDR: Poor (<5), Standard (5-12), Skilled (12-14.99), Impressive (15+)
  • SR: Low (<30%), Average (30-55%), Good (55%-70%), Impressive (70%+)
  • Raid Duration: Short (<5m), Average (5-15m), Long (15-20m), Impressive (20m+)
  • SPT Version: Outdated, Older than latest release, Latest Release
